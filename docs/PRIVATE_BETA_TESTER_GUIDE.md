# Big Al private beta

Big Al helps you keep recipes, plan meals, shop, and cook in one simple place.

Thanks for trying this early version. Some finishing touches are still in progress; we are most interested in what feels clear, useful, or difficult in real use.

## Your first session

Use the private link you received, then try this journey in your own way:

**Create account → Create Restaurant → Import → Save → Plan → Shop → Cook → Feedback**

Choose a recipe you would genuinely consider cooking. Import and save it, add it to your Menu, use the Shopping list, and open Cook Mode. You do not need to explore every screen.

## Tell us what happened

Use **Send feedback** inside Big Al whenever something gets in your way or feels worth mentioning. Short, honest notes are useful.

We would especially like to know:

- if anything blocked you or needed help;
- where wording or next steps felt confusing;
- whether the Shopping list was useful;
- whether Cook Mode helped while cooking;
- whether you would use Big Al again next week.

There are no right answers. Please describe what you expected and what happened instead.
