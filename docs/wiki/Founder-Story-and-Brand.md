# Founder Story & Brand

Big Al came from a real kitchen problem: recipes were arriving from different places and needed one useful home.

The product grew into a living cookbook focused on real cooking, planning and meals.

## Positioning

**Big Al turns scattered recipes into dinner.**

**A trusted cookbook that quietly got smarter.**

## Personality

- warm
- practical
- friendly
- relaxed
- trustworthy
- slightly witty

Humour should feel like a friend in the kitchen. Food is always the hero.
