# Product Vision & Principles

## Mission

Make cooking easy and enjoyable.

Big Al exists to make the weekly cooking loop easier. Every feature should help people collect a recipe, choose what to cook, buy ingredients, cook successfully, or remember what is worth cooking again.

## Product philosophy

Big Al is:
- a living cookbook
- a planning tool
- a shopping companion
- a cooking companion
- a recipe-centric community later

Big Al is not:
- a social media platform
- an influencer platform
- a grocery comparison engine
- a calorie tracker
- a generic AI app
- a productivity tool

## Strategic choices

### Restaurants, not Households
Restaurant is the shared cooking space. It contains members, Cookbook, Menu, Pantry and preferences.

### Recipes belong to Chefs
Attribution and provenance matter. Original creators should be credited and adaptations should preserve the original recipe.

### Community through cooking behaviour
Future community mechanics should reward useful recipes rather than popular personalities.

Prefer:
- Planned This Week
- Times Cooked
- Cook Again Rate
- Restaurants Using
- Ratings

Avoid followers, likes, view counts and influencer rankings.

### AI as assistance, not identity
AI can help with extraction, search and practical recipe help, but should not become Big Al's main identity.

## Trust principles

- Restaurants are private by default.
- Original creators should be credited.
- Adaptations should preserve the original recipe.
- Big Al should be clear when it uses saved recipe information.
- The product should avoid ad-led incentives.

## Product promise

A trusted cookbook that quietly got smarter.
