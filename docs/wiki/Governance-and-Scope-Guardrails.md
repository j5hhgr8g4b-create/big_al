# Governance & Scope Guardrails

## Decision rule

A feature belongs in Big Al only if it helps people collect a recipe, choose what to cook, buy ingredients, cook successfully, or remember what is worth cooking again.

If it does not support the cooking loop, park it.

## Do not build yet

- followers or following
- likes or view counts
- influencer ranking
- public recipe feeds
- public Chef profiles
- recipe marketplace
- grocery price comparison
- full pantry inventory management
- calorie or macro tracking
- advanced nutrition goals
- generic AI chatbot homepage
- automatic AI meal generation as the main feature
- creator monetisation
- ads or sponsored recipe promotion

## Why these are parked

They add administration or complexity, distract from cooking, change the product category, weaken the trusted-cookbook feeling, or increase cost before retained use is proven.

## Product control

Big Al must first prove that Restaurants repeatedly save recipes, plan meals, generate shopping support, cook from the app and return because the product made cooking easier.

## Current beta rule

Protect the existing core loop. Do not add speculative features during beta preparation. Use real beta evidence before choosing the next product direction.
